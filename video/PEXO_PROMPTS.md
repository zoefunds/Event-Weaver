## Prompt 1 — Scene 7 background plate (0:49–0:53, first ~4s of the mechanism scene)
- Shot purpose: abstract atmospheric texture behind the ArchitectureFlow diagram as it
  begins revealing, to give the film's most technical beat a moment of cinematic weight
  before the diagram takes over.
- Subject: none literal — abstract light and motion only, no people, no readable text, no
  UI, no crypto iconography (no coins, no chain-link cliché, no glowing blockchain cubes).
- Environment: dark, near-black gradient field.
- Exact action: slow horizontal drift of soft light particles/threads, suggesting
  independent parallel processes without depicting any specific technology literally.
- Camera framing: wide, full-bleed background plate.
- Camera movement: extremely slow lateral drift, no cuts.
- Lighting: low-key, single cool light source (Logic Blue, `#4d8eff` family) with sparse
  Adjudication Purple (`#571bc1`) accents — must read as an extension of the product's
  existing palette, not a generic sci-fi blue.
- Mood: calm, precise, quietly powerful — not ominous, not euphoric.
- Colour relationship to product branding: primary Logic Blue, secondary Adjudication
  Purple, background near-black to match the product's dark glassmorphism UI.
- Shot duration: 4 seconds.
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (with a 9:16 crop-safe center composition for the vertical cut).
- Realism level: abstract/stylized, not photoreal.
- Prohibited elements: readable text of any kind, logos, coins, padlocks, circuit-board
  clichés, humanoid figures, fake dashboards, fake UI.
- Transition compatibility: must sit fully behind Remotion-rendered diagram elements at
  reduced opacity (~30–40%) — should not compete for foreground attention once the
  ArchitectureFlow animation begins around frame +4s.
